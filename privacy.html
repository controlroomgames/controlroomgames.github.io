Privacy Policy

This policy applies to mobile games published by Control Room, including Drill Rush and Panic Zoo.

Overview

Control Room respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play games published by Control Room.

This is a unified policy for our mobile games. Some services described below may apply only when a specific game includes the related feature, such as ads, analytics, achievements, leaderboards, or platform sign-in.

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Information We Collect

We do not directly ask users to provide sensitive personal information in our games. Depending on the game, platform, and enabled features, the following types of information may be processed by us or by third-party services used in the game:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system, language, country or region, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data, when advertising is enabled.
  • Gameplay and analytics events, such as session activity, level progress, feature usage, and performance data.
  • Crash, diagnostics, and technical logs used to improve stability.
  • Platform account, achievement, leaderboard, and game progress information provided through Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center, when those features are used.
  • Purchase or transaction status provided by Google Play or the Apple App Store, when purchases are available.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate game features, provide platform services, display and measure ads, understand gameplay behavior, improve game balance and performance, prevent abuse, fix bugs, and respond to user support requests.

Advertising

Some Control Room games use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect or use device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad views, ad clicks, and other data needed to provide, personalize, limit, and measure ads.

You can manage personalized advertising settings through your device settings or platform account settings where available.

Analytics

Some Control Room games use Unity Analytics to understand how users play our games and to improve stability, balance, and content. Unity Analytics may process gameplay events, device information, session data, app version, country or region, and technical diagnostics.

Platform Services

Our games may use Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on iOS for features such as sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud or platform-linked game progress. These services are provided by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies and account settings.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use the following third-party services, depending on the platform and game features:

  • Google Play services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Apple App Store services
  • Apple Game Center
  • Unity Analytics

These third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers and platform providers only as needed to operate game features, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, store services, legal compliance, or user support.

Data Retention

Data processed by third-party services is retained according to each provider's policy and settings. We keep support emails and related information only as long as needed to respond to the request and maintain service records.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

User Choices and Data Requests

You may manage platform account, advertising, and privacy settings through your device, Google account, Apple account, Google Play Games Services, Game Center, or other applicable platform settings.

Users may request privacy-related support or deletion of data where applicable by contacting us at controlroomgames@gmail.com. Some requests may need to be handled directly through Google, Apple, Unity, or other third-party services that control the relevant account or service data.

International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries or regions outside your place of residence. Data protection laws may differ depending on where the information is processed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, services, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
