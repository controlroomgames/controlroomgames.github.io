Last Night Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Control Room handles information in Last Night for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

English 한국어

Game saves and settings

Last Night stores your progress and settings on your device. The game does not require a Control Room account or send your save files to us. It does not use in-game advertising or advertising tracking.

Unity Diagnostics

When Unity Diagnostics is enabled in your build, Unity receives an installation-specific identifier, information about app interactions and use, crash reports, performance measurements, and other technical diagnostics. Unity can associate these records with the installation identifier. Control Room uses the information to investigate errors and improve game stability. We do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Service providers and retention

Unity processes diagnostic data on behalf of Control Room and normally retains personal data in Diagnostics for 90 days by default. Processing may take place outside your country. If you email us for support, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. We keep support correspondence as long as needed to handle the request and maintain support records.

Privacy requests

You can contact Control Room at controlroomgames@gmail.com to ask about your data or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Please include the game title and the platform. We will tell you if we need more information to locate your records.

Updates

We will post changes to this policy on this page. Last updated: November 8, 2026.
